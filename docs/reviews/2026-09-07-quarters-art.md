# Cabines — panorama temático

Arte criada com a ferramenta integrada image_gen, usando `src/assets/bridge-room.webp` como referência de materiais. A composição separa duas cabines: a organização de Vasquez e os objetos pessoais de Okafor, com luz quente e desgaste coerente com a nave.

Arquivos finais: `src/assets/quarters-room.webp` (1672 × 941, 104,43 kB) e `src/assets/quarters-room-small.webp` (960 px de largura, 34,95 kB). Conversão WebP com cwebp, qualidades 84 e 80. Original preservado em `/Users/mario/.codex/generated_images/01a06ec8-8e92-7a13-8cb2-739710334fea/exec-6988c5ee-4401-4c26-91b8-b13fc7997af3.png`.

O panorama é decorativo; cofre, gravador, desenhos, números e pistas continuam nos componentes existentes. Atalhos transferem foco e rolagem aos três objetos. Luz ambiente discreta respeita movimento reduzido. Botões das rodas têm 44 × 44 px; a parede de desenhos usa duas colunas abaixo de 480 px. Sem Three.js ou dependências novas.

## Prompt final

Use case: stylized-concept. Asset: photorealistic cinematic landscape 16:9 background for DERELICT crew quarters. Reference image is STYLE AND MATERIAL reference only, not edit target. New compartment in the same abandoned industrial spacecraft. Wide eye-level photograph from the shared threshold showing two small adjoining cabins through doors wedged open: left cabin meticulously tidy, narrow made bunk, neatly folded uniform, recessed desk and plain metal cabinet; right cabin lived-in, rumpled narrow bunk, small analog reel-to-reel recorder sitting on a desk, empty enamel mug, a jacket draped on chair, scraps of children's crayon artwork seen obliquely on the far wall, abstract and too small to identify any subject or count anything. Central vertical metal partition clearly separates cabins. Chipped olive-gray steel, dull brass fittings, aged cream fabric, narrow overhead pipes, practical warm bedside lamps contrasted with faint cooler corridor light. Intimate absence, human warmth lingering in an empty ship, gentle dust in sidelight, no horror gore, no people. Medium-dark but readable, no pitch black crushed interiors. Photograph of an expensive practical film set, fine surface detail, restrained color, natural imperfections. Compose principal desks and bunks near middle height so they survive moderate top/bottom panoramic crop. No visible safe door, combination digits, keys, identifiable drawings, writing, labels, symbols, UI, watermark. No stars or windows into space. Single finished image.
