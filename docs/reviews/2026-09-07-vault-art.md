## Prompt final

Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: photorealistic cinematic environment panorama for DERELICT, grounded analog industrial spaceship horror puzzle game. Create a wide 16:9 live-action science fiction production still of the ship's sealed AI memory vault. Seen through a thick open pressure doorway, an intimate solemn cold chamber, towering sealed dark metal computer cabinets recessed along walls, central heavy memory service cabinet with its protective opaque cover closed, braided cables and coolant lines disappearing overhead, one portable service lamp casts pale silver-green light across scratched steel and brass fittings. A small open transport crate with dark foam is partly visible in foreground, contents obscured by angle and shadow. Depth through concentric structural doorway frames, subtle suspended dust, worn grating, physical tangible materials, restrained amber practical lights deep in the room, deep readable shadows, atmosphere of a dormant intelligence and an irreversible decision. Focal cabinet slightly right of center, dark lower left for caption. No humans, no faces, no holographic AI, no glowing fantasy orb, no reactor, no neon cyberpunk. No text, numbers, symbols, logos, readable displays, memory-slot arrangement, labeled columns, puzzle clues or UI. Scenery only: interactive memory rack rendered separately in code. Premium realistic cinematography, muted petroleum green and graphite with silver highlights.
