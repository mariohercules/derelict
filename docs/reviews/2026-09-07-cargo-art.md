## Prompt final

Use case: stylized-concept. Asset: cinematic photorealistic 16:9 environment background for DERELICT cargo hold. Use the industrial spaceship materials and restrained olive-gray, brass and amber palette already established in the game. A monumental enclosed freighter cargo compartment, eye-level wide 24mm photograph from a loading walkway. Tall stacks of anonymous identical battered metal shipping containers along both sides, central deep aisle, massive overhead gantry rails and crane machinery, retracted empty hook high near ceiling. Cold desaturated steel, chipped paint, dense pipework and structural ribs, dusty air cut by a few overhead practical white lights and restrained warm loading lamps. Deep physical scale, oppressive silence, long perspective, dark but legible contours, realistic practical science-fiction film set photography. Central horizontal half should retain crates, deep aisle and visible crane support during moderate top/bottom panoramic crop. No people, no open containers, no visibly special crate, no quarantine marking, no 3x3 grid, no identifiable positions, no letters or numbers, no readable labels, no hull fragment, no hidden evidence, no UI, no watermarks. No outer space windows. Image is ambience, not the puzzle board. Single finished landscape image.
